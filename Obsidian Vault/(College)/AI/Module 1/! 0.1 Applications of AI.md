### 1. Applications of AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a broad field of computer science focused on creating smart machines that can perform tasks that typically require human intelligence. AI is a growing part of everyday life, from self-driving cars to smart assistants like Siri and Alexa. Its applications span various industries and domains.

Some of the key applications include:

- **Smart Assistants:** Voice assistants like Siri, Alexa, and Google Now use AI to understand and respond to user queries.
    
- **Autonomous Vehicles:** The development of self-driving cars is a prominent application of AI, involving complex tasks like perception, navigation, and decision-making.
    
- **Chatbots:** The first chatbot, ELIZA, was created in 1966. Modern chatbots, like Eugene Goostman, have become sophisticated enough to pass the Turing test.
    
- **Game Playing:** AI has achieved superhuman performance in complex games. In 1997, IBM's Deep Blue defeated world chess champion Garry Kasparov. In 2011, IBM's Watson won the quiz show Jeopardy!, demonstrating its ability to understand natural language and solve complex questions.
    
- **Robotics:** Japan built the first intelligent humanoid robot, WABOT-1, in 1972. AI is also used in household robots, such as the Roomba vacuum cleaner, which entered homes in 2002.
    
- **Business and Social Media:** Companies like Facebook, Twitter, and Netflix started using AI around 2006 to personalize user experiences and analyze data.
    
- **Expert Systems:** These are programs that emulate the decision-making ability of a human expert. They became prominent in the 1980s.
    
- **Medical Diagnosis:** AI systems can assist doctors in diagnosing diseases by analyzing patient data and symptoms.
    
- **Image Analysis:** AI is used for tasks like categorizing satellite images.
