## Milestones
Based on the document, here are the milestones in the history of Artificial Intelligence:  
  
**Maturation of Artificial Intelligence (1943-1952)**

- **1943:** Warren McCulloch and Walter Pitts proposed a model of artificial neurons.
- **1949:** Donald Hebb demonstrated Hebbian learning, an updating rule for modifying connection strength between neurons.
- **1950:** Alan Turing published "Computing Machinery and Intelligence," proposing the Turing Test.

**The birth of Artificial Intelligence (1952-1956)**

- **1955:** Allen Newell and Herbert A. Simon created the "Logic Theorist," the first artificial intelligence program.
- **1956:** The term "Artificial Intelligence" was first adopted by John McCarthy at the Dartmouth Conference, coining AI as an academic field.

**The golden years - Early enthusiasm (1956-1974)**

- **1966:** Joseph Weizenbaum created the first chatbot, ELIZA.
- **1972:** The first intelligent humanoid robot, WABOT-1, was built in Japan.

**The first AI winter (1974-1980)**

- This period saw a severe shortage of government funding for AI research and decreased public interest.

**A boom of AI (1980-1987)**

- **1980:** AI came back with "Expert Systems," programs that emulated human expert decision-making.
- **1980:** The first national conference of the American Association of Artificial Intelligence was held.

**The second AI winter (1987-1993)**

- Investors and government again stopped funding AI research due to high costs and inefficient results.

**The emergence of intelligent agents (1993-2011)**

- **1997:** IBM Deep Blue beat world chess champion Gary Kasparov.
- **2002:** AI entered homes with Roomba, a vacuum cleaner.
- **2006:** Companies like Facebook, Twitter, and Netflix started using AI.

**Deep learning, big data and artificial general intelligence (2011-present)**

- **2011:** IBM's Watson won Jeopardy, a quiz show, demonstrating natural language understanding and quick problem-solving.
- **2012:** Google launched "Google now," an Android app feature providing predictive information.
- **2014:** Chatbot "Eugene Goostman" won a competition in the Turing test.
- **2018:** IBM's "Project Debater" debated complex topics with master debaters.
- **2018:** Google demonstrated "Duplex," a virtual assistant that made a hairdresser appointment without the person on the other end realizing they were talking to a machine.
- The concepts of Deep learning, big data, and data science are now trending.
