AI can be described using 4 **definitions**

### Acting Humanly
[[1.1.1 Turing Test]]

- A computer is said to act like a human if it passes the Turing test 

### Thinking Humanly
- To say a program thinks like a human we need to know how a human thinks
	- **Introspection** : trying to catch our own thoughts as they go by
	- **Psychological** **Experimentation** : observing a person in action
	- **Brain** **Imaging** : observing the brain in action

	- If the programs input-output behavior matches that of human behavior
