Now that you have a solid grasp on how block ciphers like AES work under the hood, we can move on to the second major category of cryptography. This topic is an absolute favorite for KTU examiners, particularly for numerical problems.

### **Topic 3: Asymmetric Cryptography and the RSA Algorithm**

**The Concept (How to understand it):**

Unlike symmetric encryption where one key does everything, asymmetric encryption uses a **Public Key** to lock the message and a mathematically related **Private Key** to unlock it.

The most famous algorithm for this is **RSA** (named after its inventors Rivest, Shamir, and Adleman). The entire security of RSA relies on the **integer factorization problem**. In simple terms: it is very easy for a computer to multiply two giant prime numbers together, but it is practically impossible for a computer to take that massive result and figure out what the original two prime numbers were.

**The 4 Steps of RSA Key Generation:**

1. **Modulus Generation:** Pick two large prime numbers, $p$ and $q$. Multiply them to get the modulus $n$ ($n = p \times q$).
    
2. **Generate Co-prime:** Find a number $e$ that is a co-prime to $(p-1)(q-1)$. This means no number other than 1 can evenly divide both $e$ and $(p-1)(q-1)$.
    
3. **Public Key:** The public key is the pair $\{e, n\}$.
    
4. **Private Key:** Calculate $d$, which is the inverse of $e$ modulo $(p-1)(q-1)$. The formula is $ed = 1 \mod (p-1)(q-1)$. The private key is $\{d, n\}$.
    

**Encryption & Decryption:**

- **Encryption:** $Ciphertext (C) = P^e \mod n$ _(where P is the Plaintext)_.
    
- **Decryption:** $Plaintext (P) = C^d \mod n$.

**PYQ 1: Outline the concepts to achieve cryptography using RSA algorithm. (May 2024 - 9 Marks)**

    - **Encryption & Decryption (3 Marks):** * The sender encrypts the plaintext $P$ using the receiver's public key $e$ and modulus $n$: $C = P^e \mod n$.
        
        - The receiver decrypts the ciphertext $C$ using their private key $d$ and modulus $n$: $P = C^d \mod n$.
            

**PYQ 2: Explain public and private keys. Perform encryption and decryption using RSA algorithm for p=3, q=11, e=7 and M=5. (Oct 2023 - 7 Marks)**

- **Answer:**
    
    - **Definitions (2 Marks):** A public key is placed in an accessible register and used by anyone to encrypt messages intended for the owner. The private key is kept secret by the owner and is used to decrypt those incoming messages.
        
    - **Step 1: Find $n$ (1 Mark)**
        
        - $n = p \times q = 3 \times 11 = 33$
            
    - **Step 2: Find Euler's Totient $\phi(n)$ (1 Mark)**
        
        - $\phi(n) = (p-1)(q-1) = (3-1)(11-1) = 2 \times 10 = 20$
            
    - **Step 3: Find Private Key $d$ (1 Mark)**
        
        - $e \times d = 1 \mod \phi(n)$
            
        - $7 \times d = 1 \mod 20$
            
        - _(Testing multipliers of 20, plus 1, that are divisible by 7: 21 is divisible by 7)_
            
        - $7 \times 3 = 21$. Therefore, **$d = 3$**.
            
        - Public Key = $\{7, 33\}$, Private Key = $\{3, 33\}$.
            
    - **Step 4: Encryption (1 Mark)**
        
        - $C = M^e \mod n$
            
        - $C = 5^7 \mod 33$
            
        - $C = 78125 \mod 33 = 14$. **(Ciphertext = 14)**
            
    - **Step 5: Decryption (1 Mark)**
        
        - $M = C^d \mod n$
            
        - $M = 14^3 \mod 33$
            
        - $M = 2744 \mod 33 = 5$. **(Decrypted back to Original Message = 5)**
