## 10. Log-Based Rollback Recovery

### Idea:

Log non-deterministic events to replay them after failure.

### Steps:

1. Log events
2. On failure, restore checkpoint
3. Replay logs

### Types:

- **Pessimistic logging** → logs immediately (safe, slow)
- **Optimistic logging** → logs later (fast, risky)
- **Causal logging** → balance between both
