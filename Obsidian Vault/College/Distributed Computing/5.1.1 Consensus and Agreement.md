### Core Assumptions (Write EXACT points)

1. **Failure Model**

- At most _f faulty processes_
- Types:
    - Fail-stop
    - Omission failures
    - Byzantine failures

2. **Synchronous vs Asynchronous**

- Synchronous → message delay bounded (detect failures)
- Asynchronous → cannot distinguish delay vs failure

3. **Network Connectivity**

- Fully connected system (any process → any process)

4. **Sender Identification**

- Receiver knows who sent message

5. **Channel Reliability**

- Messages not corrupted/lost (only processes fail)

6. **Authentication**

- Authenticated → forgery detectable
- Unauthenticated → messages can be tampered

7. **Agreement Variable**

- Can be Boolean or multi-valued
