To create a safe working environment, two main steps are taken:

1. **Elimination**: If possible, eliminate the causes of accidents entirely.
    
2. **Protection**: If elimination is not possible, arrange to shield the hazardous area using guards, enclosures, or similar protective measures.

## Need For Safety
1. Protect health of all employees
2. Ensure well-being of all workers
3. Promote positive culture
4. Ensure accident and incident prevention
5. Ensure organizational development and growth
6. Ensure continual improvement of the company
