## Conventional
### Fossil Fuels
- Coal
	  conversion of vegetable matter
	  mainly carbon and hydrocarbons
	  heated in furnace to make coke
	  heat from coal used to make plastics, tar, synthetics
- Natural Gas
	  mainly methane with small amount propane and ethane
	  colorless, odorless, cleanest fossil fuel
	  found underground with petroleum deposits
- Petroleum
- Gas
	  incompletely utilized currently because of high transport cost, large reserve located in inaccessible area
	  fixed composition : acetylene, ethylene, methane
	  composite industrial gases : producer gas, coke oven gas, blast furnace gas

### Disadvantages
- unreliable supply
- pollution
- damage to animal life
- high cost
