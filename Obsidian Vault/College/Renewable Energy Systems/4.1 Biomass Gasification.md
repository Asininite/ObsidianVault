### **Topic 1: Biomass Gasification**

Gasification is a key thermo-chemical method for converting solid biomass into a more versatile gaseous fuel.

#### **For a 3-Mark Question**

A short-answer question would focus on the definition, product, and basic principles.

**Sample Question (3 Marks): What is biomass gasification? What is the primary fuel produced, and what are the main types of gasifiers?**

Biomass gasification is a thermo-chemical process that converts solid biomass fuel (like wood chips or agricultural residue) into a combustible gaseous fuel111111111. This conversion is achieved through partial combustion of the biomass in a reactor with a restricted supply of air or oxygen2222.

- **Primary Fuel Produced:** The main product is a combustible gas called **producer gas** or **syngas**, which is a mixture of carbon monoxide (CO), hydrogen (![](data:,)), and methane (![](data:,))333333333.
    
- **Main Types of Gasifiers:** Gasifiers are broadly classified into **fixed bed gasifiers** (which include downdraft, updraft, and crossdraft types) and **fluidized bed gasifiers**4444.

#### **For a 7-Mark Question**

A long-answer question requires a detailed explanation of the process, including the different zones and chemical reactions, often with a diagram.

**Sample Question (7 Marks): With a neat sketch, explain the working of a downdraft gasifier and detail the chemical reactions occurring in its different zones.**

A downdraft gasifier is a type of fixed-bed reactor where the biomass fuel and the gasifying agent (air) move in the same downward direction (co-current flow)5. This design is widely used because it produces a cleaner gas with low tar content, as volatile compounds are forced to pass through the high-temperature combustion zone where they are cracked into simpler gases6666.

Construction and Working:

1. **Drying Zone (100–150°C):** At the top of the gasifier, heat rising from the lower zones dries the incoming biomass, driving off its moisture content as water vapor (steam)77777.
