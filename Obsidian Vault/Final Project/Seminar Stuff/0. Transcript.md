- Traditional audit methods reveal significant shortcomings: they often rely on outdated systems vulnerable to security breaches, unauthorized access, and manual procedures prone to errors and delays. Furthermore, these systems are typically time-consuming and costly with absence of real time verification

- To address these challenges, blockchain technology offers a promising solution for maintaining reliable audit trails and enhancing audit practices.

- transactions recorded on blockchain are immutable and visible to all participants, significantly reducing the risk of fraud and enhancing the overall efficiency of the audit process.

- our objective was to develop an efficient approach for finding verification information within a blockchain for the audit trail. This information is required to verify the integrity and authenticity of data stored in the blockchain. 
	  The study sought to introduce an optimized approach that both ensures efficient verification and also integrates it into the design of a comprehensive audit trail framework. 
		  This framework will enhance the overall efficiency and scalability of blockchain-based audit systems, ensuring they remain practical for real-world applications while maintaining their core properties of immutability, security, and availability.

- This research focuses solely on verifying a specific piece of information within the blockchain, rather than locating or searching for it. The primary objective is to ensure that the information can be quickly and reliably verified as part of the blockchain, which addresses one of the critical aspects of blockchain audit systems. By narrowing our focus to verification rather than searchability, this work aims to develop an approach that is both efficient and practical for real-world applications.

- To verify the presence of data on the blockchain, a typical approach involves searching for and locating the data. However, if the purpose of finding this data is only for verification, a more efficient solution could be employed using cryptographic accumulators.

- both time and space efficient, making them reliable and effective solution for this purpose 

- cryptographic accumulators emerge as a powerful solution for transaction verification. Unlike traditional methods, the cryptographic accumulator allows transaction hashes to be securely accumulated into a compact data structure, enabling efficient membership verification without the need to process the entire blockchain. This not only enhances the scalability of blockchain systems but also ensures robust and tamper-resistant auditing.

- An audit trail is a record of all the events and activities that occur within a particular system. It provides a chronological order of events that allows someone to trace the history of a transaction or an activity from its inception to its current state. These transactions or activities may be scientific, financial transactions or communication by individuals, system, or other entities

1. External Audit: is an independent examination of an organization’s or department’s operational practices by a third-party auditor. It provides an unbiased assessment of the entity’s internal controls and compliance with relevant standards and regulations 
2) Internal Audit: is conducted by an organization’s own internal audit team. It enables an organization/ department to examine its progress and implement measures to foster future expansion. It also identifies and mitigates risks and ensures compliance with internal policies and procedures
